# VSTEP B1 Vocabulary Game

Day la web app tu hoc tu vung tieng Anh theo 8 nhom lon va topic nho thiet thuc cho VSTEP B1, tap trung vao:

- Nghia tieng Viet
- Phien am IPA
- Vi du ngan de tap noi va viet
- Nut nghe phat am bang giong may
- Chon voice tieng Anh tren may de uu tien chat luong doc tot hon
- Luyen noi thu neu trinh duyet ho tro nhan dien giong noi
- 5 che do hoc: flashcard, trac nghiem, go tu, phat am, on theo cau hoi VSTEP
- Lo trinh hoc theo 8 nhom lon + topic nho
- Bo loc `Speaking / Writing / Both` de hoc dung muc tieu
- Cac goi hoc 300, 500, 700, 1000, 1500 va 2000 tu dang hoc duoc ngay

## Cach mo

1. Mo file [index.html](/Users/thamkhang/Downloads/vode/index.html) bang Chrome, Edge hoac Safari.
2. Chon nhom lon, topic nho, muc tieu hoc va che do hoc.
3. Chon `Giong doc` va `Toc do doc` o thanh cong cu.
4. Bam `Nghe thu giong` de test voice.
5. Bam `Nghe phat am` hoac `Nghe mau` de nghe tu.
6. Trong che do `Phat am`, bam `Noi thu` neu trinh duyet ho tro microphone.

## Goi y hoc nhanh

1. Hoc theo tung nhom lon, sau do di vao topic nho de on sat dang cau hoi VSTEP.
2. Moi tu di theo 1 vong: xem nghia, doc IPA, nghe mau, noi lai, dat 1 cau rieng.
3. Sau do chuyen sang `Trac nghiem` va `Go tu` de khoa nho.
4. Cuoi cung luyen `Phat am` de chuan bi cho phan noi.

## Ghi chu ve do chuan TTS

Ban hien tai van dang dung browser TTS nen chat luong se phu thuoc vao voice co san tren may.
Neu muon nhay len mot muc "giong nguoi that" ro ret, can them cloud TTS hoac file audio da tao san.
